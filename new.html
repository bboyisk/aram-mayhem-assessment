How the Rating Works

Based on each player's own last 100 ARAM Mayhem games, remakes under 5 minutes excluded. There is no together-games filter: every player is scored on their own last 100 games. Per-game grades are percentiles within the player's role archetype (Damage / Frontline / Support), so tanks and supports are not judged on carry stats. This page reports only numbers backed by the match data — every score below traces to a stat the match records contain.

The eight categories

Overall = (1 − w) × Performance + w × Winning Impact  ·  Performance blends the seven graded categories below

  • Winning Impact — weight 20 (the toggle at the top selects 10 / 20 / 30). The one category about results rather than in-game play. Every champion has a population win rate in ARAM Mayhem (MayhemMeta, current patch), and that rate is the expectation for each game: winning on a 58% champion is expected, winning on a 44% champion beats the odds. Expected wins are added up across all 100 games and compared with the player's actual wins, and the gap is converted to 0–100 on a standard curve — 50 means exactly as many wins as the champions predict, and near the middle of the scale each extra win above expectation is worth roughly 8 points, so four extra wins land in the high 70s. A carried win counts the same as a carry win; how well the player actually played is what Performance measures.
  • Team Damage % — 20. The player's share of their own team's damage dealt, graded within their archetype. Each card also shows the player's actual average share.
  • Frontline Absorption — 15. Share of the team's damage taken, scored only in frontline (tank/bruiser) games; in other games the category is skipped and the remaining performance weights renormalize. A game whose deaths-per-minute lands in the frontline pool's worst quartile has its absorption score capped, so running in and dying is not rewarded. Each card also shows the player's actual average damage-taken share in those games.
  • Gold per Minute — 7.5. Gold earned per minute, graded within the archetype. Each card shows the player's actual average gold per minute.
  • Build — 7.5. How the final build is rated, out of 100, in four parts. Champion synergy (35): the share of the build's gold spent on items matching the champion's damage profile — gold split across profiles scores low. Countering the enemy team (30): the checks that apply to that game, averaged — an anti-heal item when the enemy team has two or more healers scores 100 (missing it scores 30; buying anti-heal against at most one enemy healer scores 40, gold spent on a non-problem), and a percent-penetration item when two or more enemies built tank scores 100 (missing it scores 35). Team gap (20): against two or more enemy healers, being the team's only anti-heal source scores 100, sharing the job scores 85, and leaving it to a teammate scores 70; if nobody on the team bought anti-heal, the part is skipped rather than charged to one player. Efficiency (15): the share of items completed rather than left as components, minus 10 if the build ends with no boots. Any part that doesn't apply to a game is skipped and the rest are re-weighted to 100. Every deduction carries a reason — the most frequent ones appear as chips on each card.
  • KDA — 10. (Kills + assists) ÷ deaths, ratio capped, graded within the archetype.
  • Augments — 10. How the Augments score is rated: every augment has a population win rate across ARAM Mayhem games (MayhemMeta, current patch). A pick's score is that win rate's percentile among all augments — a pick whose win rate beats 80% of augments scores 80 — and a game's Augments score is the average across its picks. The score is not adjusted for the champion played or the team comps: it rates whether the player tends to end up on augments that win in the population data. Picks missing from the population data (new or renamed augments) are scored a neutral 50; each card notes how many of that player's picks were missing.
  • Kill Participation — 10. (Kills + assists) ÷ team kills. Each card also shows the player's actual average participation.

What the data cannot show

  • Augments: only the pick is visible, and only its population strength is scored. Match data records the augment a player picked — never the other options they were offered — so choosing well among three options cannot be measured. The score rates the picked augment's population win rate, not the decision among alternatives.
  • Item Fit scores final builds only. The data shows the build a game ended with, not when each item was bought, so purchase timing and order are not scored.
  • Healing, shielding and crowd control are not published per player for this mode, which is why Frontline Absorption (damage-taken share in frontline games) stands in as the durability measure.

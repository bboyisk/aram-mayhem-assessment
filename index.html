      How the Rating Works

      Based on each player’s last 50 ARAM Mayhem games on PlayARAM — 300 player-games across 147 unique games, with remakes under 5 minutes excluded. There is no together-games filter: every player is scored on their own last 50 games, so each player has exactly 50 games. Every game is graded 0–100 per criterion against players on the same role archetype (Damage / Frontline / Support), with percentiles computed across the 300 player-games in the dataset, so tanks and supports are not judged on carry stats.

      The seven criteria

      • Output — blends per-minute rates with the player's share of their own in-game team's totals. For Damage: 50% damage-per-minute percentile + 50% team damage-share percentile. For Frontline: 55% damage-taken part (half taken per minute, half team taken-share) + 45% damage part (the same blend as Damage). For Support: 55% assists per minute + 45% damage part (the same blend).
      • KDA efficiency — kills, deaths and assists weighed against the archetype's expected line.
      • Teamplay — kill participation: (kills + assists) ÷ team kills.
      • Death quality — deaths per minute (Frontline also credited for damage soaked per death), blended with late-game death timing taken from each match's timeline.
      • Conversion — the percentile, within the player's archetype, of their share of their own team's structure takedowns: credited turrets and inhibitors divided by the team's total that game. Turning fights into structures, measured as a share rather than a raw count.
      • Augments — how the augments that player drafted performed across the whole group's 300 player-games in this dataset (win rate + KDA, augments seen at least 3 times).
      • Mechanics — skillshots dodged per minute, as shown on PlayARAM summary cards. Dodges only.

      Each player card's radar chart plots that player's seven criteria subscores against the group average across the same seven criteria, so strengths and gaps are visible at a glance. Radar scale: zoomed — the center is 30 and the outer ring is 65.9, the highest subscore in the group (Vincent Chan's Output), so all six radars share one scale and the differences are easier to see.

      From grades to a rating

      The overall assigned score is the Performance Score (Mayhem Performance Rating) — the number shown in the ranking strip, the summary table, and at the top of each player card.

      Performance Score (Mayhem Performance Rating) = 55% mean game grade + 20% median grade + 10% floor (25th percentile) grade + 15% win rate

      MMR-style = 800 + Performance Score × 14  —  1500 is a roughly average performance

      The median and floor terms are the consistency check: a player whose worst games crater scores lower than one with the same average and a higher floor.

      Champion focus

      Each player card's Champion focus block compares the player's mean game grade on each champion they have played at least 3 times against their own overall mean grade. Champions grading 4 or more points above that average go on the Take when offered list; champions 4 or more points below go on Pass or swap away — in ARAM champions are dealt, so this is guidance for bench decisions, not a pick order. Samples are small (3–8 games per champion here), so treat it as directional draft guidance, not a verdict.

      Population baselines

      Each card's expected record sums the champion population win rate for every game that player was dealt — if the champions they received win 48.7% of the time population-wide, a 50-game set is expected to go about 24–26 — and the card shows how many wins they finished above or below that expectation. The champion population win rates shown in Champion focus, and the Augment checks lists, come from MayhemMeta (mayhemmeta.com), snapshot fetched Nov 4, 2026 on the current patch. Trap picks are augments the player took 3 or more times whose population win rate is at most 48%; Proven picks are augments taken 3 or more times at 52.5% or better — augment rates rest on large samples (tens of thousands of games per augment). A champion can grade well for a player while being weak population-wide (a genuine pocket pick), or the reverse. MayhemMeta does not document where its data comes from and its champion table publishes no game counts, so these baselines are directional context only — they are not part of the Performance Score.

      What isn't measured

      PlayARAM does not publish healing, shielding, crowd-control score or per-player objective damage, so support utility is approximated by assists, participation and damage taken — enchanter-heavy players (notably pikabUwU) are likely understated. Ratings cluster in the 40s–50s because every criterion is a percentile grade against the same archetype pool.

      Share of group damage is each player's damage dealt to champions across the 300 player-games in this dataset as a percentage of the six players' combined total. Kill participation (KP) = (kills + assists) ÷ team kills.
